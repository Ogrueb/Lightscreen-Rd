# 2026-11-09: the HDR fix ships as 3.0.2, not a re-tagged 3.0.1

- Supersedes `2026-11-08-bug-9-retag-3-0-1.md`. That decision assumed the 3.0.1 release (tag `v3.0.1` on `4986f90`) was still an unpublished draft. It had been published on 2026-11-08, so the update check already offers it, and moving a published tag would change what people who downloaded 3.0.1 can trace it to.
- The owner said to move to 3.0.2 if reusing 3.0.1 was a problem. `master` (PR #67 and later) is released as 3.0.2; `v3.0.1` and its release stay as they are.
- CHANGELOG keeps a short 3.0.1 entry (the first, incomplete HDR fix) and gives the full fix, option, report, safeguards and known issues under 3.0.2. `docs/release-notes/v3.0.1.md` is restored to the text 3.0.1 shipped with; the new text is `docs/release-notes/v3.0.2.md`.
